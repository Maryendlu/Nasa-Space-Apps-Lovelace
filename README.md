*About the Project*

*Mars Academy* is an educational and interactive game aimed at children. In the story, after planet Earth collapses, the player takes on the role of one of the astronauts on a space mission and must survive for ten days on Mars, facing real-life challenges and phenomena related to life on the Red Planet.

*Our Goal*

We created *Mars Academy* to spark children's interest in science and space exploration in a fun and educational way. We aim to foster critical thinking, teach the management of scarce resources, and show the real impact of every choice — always reinforcing that caring for and preserving our planet Earth is the most important mission of all.

---

*How the Game Works*

* *Vital Indicators Management:* Throughout the mission, the player must balance six indicators: Oxygen, Water, Food, Energy, Radiation, and Health.
* *Educational Consequences:* Every decision alters the indicators. If any resource or health reaches 0%, or if radiation reaches 100%, the mission ends.
* *Night Exploration:* Between mission days, the astronaut can explore the base and the Martian environment, completing up to 3 tasks per night to earn rewards that go into the inventory and help restore indicators.
* *Real-Time Emergencies:* On three random nights per playthrough, critical time-limited emergencies occur. Failing to resolve them in time also ends the mission.

---

*Science and Real Data*

To ensure educational accuracy, the trivia, space phenomena, and scientific explanations presented throughout the game are based on official NASA sources and integrate public solar activity data obtained through the **NASA DONKI API* (Space Weather Database Of Notifications, Knowledge, Information).*
